Our Story

A kitchen guided by season & soul

Savor began as a twelve-seat counter with one idea: cook honestly with what the land gives us. Today we still source from the same small farms, still hand-roll our pastas, and still greet most guests by name.

Our menu changes with the harvest. Plates are built around a single hero ingredient, then layered with technique gathered across three continents. The result is food that feels both familiar and entirely new.

  • ✓ Farm-to-table sourcing, daily deliveries
  • ✓ Seasonal menu rewritten every 10 weeks
  • ✓ Vegetarian, vegan & gluten-free options
  • ✓ Private dining & chef's table experiences
